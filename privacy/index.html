Privacy policy

This policy describes how Pactelis Whiteboard handles information on your device, and what happens when you choose to share files or visit an external website.

Local by design. No account is required. The application does not send your boards to Pactelis, and it has no analytics, telemetry, or advertising.

1. Information stored on your device

Pactelis Whiteboard, provided by Pactelis, stores boards and application data locally on your device. This includes board titles, drawings, text, notes, imported images, thumbnail previews, settings, recovery snapshots, and local backups. Your boards may contain personal information if you choose to add it.

The application also keeps local diagnostic logs for operational events and errors. These may contain file paths and error details. The application does not automatically upload these logs.

The application does not provide application-level encryption or password protection for local boards. Access to local files depends on your device, Windows account, and operating-system protections.

2. Accounts, analytics, and network use

Pactelis Whiteboard does not require an account. Ordinary drawing, editing, searching, saving, importing, and exporting work offline.

  • The application does not use analytics or telemetry.
  • It contains no advertising or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not automatically upload boards to cloud services.
  • It does not provide cloud synchronization or remote board sharing.
  • It does not track support clicks or contributions.

Pactelis does not operate a server that stores users' whiteboards. Windows, the Microsoft Store, your browser, and other software may handle their own information under their respective privacy practices. This policy describes what Pactelis Whiteboard does; it does not make promises about every temporary file or operating-system activity.

3. Import, export, images, and clipboard

Import and export actions are initiated by you. The application accesses files you select to import boards or images and writes files to the destinations you choose when exporting. Imported content is processed locally; imported HTML or SVG is treated as data, without running its scripts or loading remote resources.

Exports and backups can contain board content and embedded images. They remain where you save them. If you share those files, save them to a cloud-synchronized folder, or use another application to upload them, that is separate from Pactelis Whiteboard's local storage behavior.

Copy and paste use the Windows clipboard. Other software with clipboard access may read content you copy. Review what you export or share if your board contains personal information.

4. Optional support for development

If a distributed build includes the Support development / Buy Me a Coffee link, choosing it only opens Pactelis on Buy Me a Coffee, an external HTTPS website, in your browser. The default Microsoft Store build keeps this link disabled.

Payment information is never handled by Pactelis Whiteboard. The external provider handles any transaction. The application does not collect payment credentials, process payments, or record payment status.

Contributions are voluntary. They unlock no features and do not create a premium entitlement. All application functionality is available regardless of whether you contribute.

External websites and your browser are governed by their own privacy policies and may receive the ordinary information associated with a website visit. Review the provider's policy before using its services.

5. Data retention and your choices

Your boards and application data remain in local storage until you remove them or the operating system removes the relevant storage. Pactelis does not hold a server-side copy of your whiteboards.

Deleting a board does not guarantee immediate removal of every retained backup, recovery snapshot, or associated asset. These local copies can support recovery. Separate exports and backups remain where you saved them.

To remove local information, close the application and remove its local data and any separate exports or backups you want to delete. Export important boards first if you want to keep them. Windows may remove package-managed data when a Microsoft Store installation is uninstalled or reset. Removing a portable program does not automatically remove its separate data.

Keep an exported copy of important boards outside application storage before uninstalling, resetting, or moving between distribution types. Local backups alone do not protect against device or drive loss. Operating-system caches, clipboard contents, and copies created by other software are controlled separately.

6. This website

These public pages use no analytics, tracking scripts, advertising, forms, or embedded payment tools. The site files do not set cookies or use browser storage.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub handles hosting requests under its own privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a separate website with its own privacy practices.

7. Contact and policy updates

A public privacy and application-support email has not yet been designated. Contact details will be added here before the Store submission is finalized. The Buy Me a Coffee link is for voluntary contributions, rather than a designated privacy or application-support contact.

This policy will be updated if the application's data handling changes. The date at the top identifies the latest update.
