Support information

A few useful answers about keeping your boards, moving your work, and supporting development.

Where is my work saved?

Boards and application data save locally on your Windows device. Pactelis Whiteboard does not provide a cloud account or cloud synchronization.

Export important boards to a location outside application storage. Windows may remove package-managed data when a Store installation is uninstalled or reset. Keep another copy if a board matters to you.

How do I move boards to another device?

Export a portable board package from the application, transfer the file yourself, and import it into Pactelis Whiteboard on the other device. Review files before sharing them: exports can include your board content and images.

Can I support development?

Optional contributions are available through Pactelis on Buy Me a Coffee. This link opens an external HTTPS website. The provider handles payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Pactelis Whiteboard never handles your payment information. Contributions unlock no features or premium entitlement. You can use every application feature without contributing.

Privacy or application-support questions

A public contact email has not yet been designated. Contact details will be added before the Store submission is finalized. The contribution page is not a designated support channel.
